Research

I develop knowledge-informed machine learning for complex scientific and physical systems. In medicine, agriculture, and manufacturing, labeled data are expensive, every patient, field, or production line differs, and wrong decisions are costly. Instead of asking AI to learn everything from data, I design models that start from what domain experts already know and use data to learn the rest.

  • Knowledge as a prior. Anatomy, soil–water–crop dynamics, and process structure are built into model architectures, training objectives, and initialization.
  • Modern AI as the engine. Foundation models, LLMs, generative models, and world models provide flexible capacity.
  • Decisions as the goal. Models are evaluated by the quality of the diagnoses and control actions they enable.

Research Thrusts

01

Perception from scarce data

Semi-supervised learning · generative models · foundation models · 3D medical imaging

A clinician can spot a lesion on a 3D scan because they know where teeth, bone, and nerves should be. I give AI that same anatomical understanding so it can learn from a small number of expert-labeled scans.

This includes anatomy-informed semi-supervised segmentation and lesion detection in dental cone-beam CT, anatomy-normalized radiomics for differential diagnosis, anatomy-informed pseudo-healthy reconstruction of brain MRI, and foundation-model cascades for zero-shot microscopy analysis. Two of these methods are the subject of U.S. provisional patent applications.

  • Oral-anatomical knowledge-informed semi-supervised learning for 3D dental CBCT segmentation and lesion detection, IEEE T-ASE, 2025
  • Dentin-normalized CBCT radiomics for differentiating granulomas and radicular cysts, J. Endodontics, in press, 2026
  • BrainNormalizer: anatomy-informed pseudo-healthy brain reconstruction, IISE Trans. Healthcare Sys. Eng., 2026
  • Foundation model cascades for zero-shot microscopy image analysis, Cytotherapy, 2026
02

Reasoning over scientific signals

LLMs · spatiotemporal learning · anomaly detection

Soil sensors produce streams of numbers that only an expert can interpret. I build models that read these signals like an agronomist, recognizing what is happening in the soil and what it means for the crop.

This includes LLM-based frameworks for soil-moisture pattern recognition and anomaly detection, spatiotemporal learning of depth-specific soil-moisture trajectories for cross-year yield prediction, and lightweight anomaly detection for on-site nutrient management.

  • SPADE: an LLM framework for soil-moisture pattern recognition and anomaly detection, Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, under review
  • Lightweight anomaly detection for sustainable nutrient management, ACS ES&T Engineering, 2026
03

Decisions under environment shifts

World models · meta-learning · test-time adaptation · model predictive control

Before irrigating, a good farmer imagines how the crop will respond. A world model plays that role for an AI controller. My work lets it adapt to a new farm by reading a description of its soil and climate and then refining itself from a few sensor readings.

An action-conditioned world model learns shared soil–water–crop dynamics, and a lightweight adapter captures what is specific to each environment. An LLM encodes environmental knowledge, a hypernetwork turns it into the adapter’s initialization, meta-learning with factual and counterfactual irrigation responses trains the adaptation, and test-time adaptation refines it from sparse observations before model predictive control. Theory characterizes the role of prior knowledge under partial identifiability and bounds decision regret by prediction error.

  • Knowledge-informed world model adaptation for predictive irrigation control under environmental shifts, IISE Transactions, under review

Applications

Precision diagnosis

Lesion segmentation, differential diagnosis, and treatment-outcome prediction from 3D dental CBCT, and pseudo-healthy reconstruction of brain MRI, developed with clinical collaborators.

Sustainable agriculture

Soil-moisture interpretation, yield prediction, nutrient management, and irrigation control that adapts to new soils and climates.

Intelligent manufacturing

Automated quality assessment of cell-therapy manufacturing from microscopy, and discovery of fault-introducing tool groups in printed circuit board production.

Future Directions

From simulation to the field

Validating adaptive world models on working farms, with remote sensing and plant-based measurements to keep adaptation reliable under sparse observations.

Scientific world models across systems

Extending knowledge-conditioned adaptation across crops, sites, and other physical systems so one model can quickly specialize to many environments.

AI for clinical decisions

Moving from detecting disease to predicting treatment outcomes from 3D imaging to support clinical decision-making.
